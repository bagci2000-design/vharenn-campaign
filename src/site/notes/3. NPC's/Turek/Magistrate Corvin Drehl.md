The lead of [[2. Regions/Landmarks & Wonders/Turek\|Turek]]'s [[1. Vharren World Lore/Factions/Frostbrand Tribunal\|Frostbrand Tribunal]] branch. Once a comfortable bureaucrat in Frostbrand’s heartlands, Corvin was reassigned to the island as punishment for incompetence. He cares little for politics, more for surviving long enough to retire with his life intact. Cowardly but observant, he often provides useful gossip from behind his paperwork if prompted correctly. Drehl does not strike you as cruel for cruelty’s sake. He believes deeply in hierarchy, procedure and the authority of the [[1. Vharren World Lore/Factions/Frostbrand Tribunal\|Frostbrand Tribunal]]—especially when that authority protects his own position. He is not a brave man. His loyalty to law appears much stronger when he is safely behind soldiers, titles and institutions. You get the impression that revenge is secondary to him. What he really wants is his authority restored, his humiliation corrected, and the people who attacked him publicly placed beneath the law again. He would rather see you arrested, judged and punished through Tribunal authority than personally butchered in the road. But if the law gave him permission to have you executed, you doubt he would lose any sleep over it. Most importantly: **Drehl is predictable.** He values survival, status and legitimacy. He is unlikely to take a reckless action unless he believes the Tribunal stands behind him.
