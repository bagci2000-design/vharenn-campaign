## Death Penalties

When a player character dies, the following penalties apply:

### XP Loss

- The character loses XP up to a maximum of **50% of their current XP progress in their current level**.
    
- This XP loss cannot reduce the character below the beginning of their current level.
    
- A character cannot lose a level as a result of death.
    

### Death Disadvantage

- The character has **disadvantage on all rolls** for **two weeks of in-game time**.
    
- Additional deaths cause the duration to **stack**.
    
- Each death adds another two weeks to the remaining duration.
    

> **Example:** A character with one week of Death Disadvantage remaining dies again. Their remaining duration becomes three weeks.

## Revival

- A dead player character will always be revived.
    
- Revival will always take place before the end of the **first half of the next session** following the character’s death.
    
- The exact circumstances of the revival are determined by the Dungeon Master.
    
- Once revived, the character returns with **15% of their maximum hit points**, rounded up.
    
- The character retains any spell slots, class resources, exhaustion, conditions, or other resources they had at the time of death unless the Dungeon Master rules otherwise.
    

## Injury Rules

Injuries are separated into two categories when a character dies.

### Injuries From the Fatal Encounter

- Injuries sustained during the encounter that directly led to the character’s death are removed when the character is revived.
    
- These injuries do not contribute towards permanent injury thresholds or lasting injury progression.
    
- This represents the character’s body being restored as part of the revival process.
    

### Pre-Existing Injuries

- Injuries sustained before the fatal encounter remain after revival.
    
- These injuries continue to count towards permanent injury thresholds.
    
- Revival does not cure or reset existing long-term or permanent injuries.
    

## Rules Summary

|Consequence|Effect|
|---|---|
|XP loss|Lose up to 50% of the XP required for the current level|
|Level loss|Characters cannot lose a level|
|Roll penalty|Disadvantage on all rolls|
|Penalty duration|Two in-game weeks per death|
|Stacking|Each death adds another two weeks|
|Revival timing|First half of the following session|
|Revival HP|25% of maximum HP, rounded up|
|Fatal encounter injuries|Removed and do not count towards permanent injury|
|Pre-existing injuries|Remain and continue to count|
