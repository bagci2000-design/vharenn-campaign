Characters may split from the party to pursue independent objectives. The rules governing separation depend on whether the characters are inside a **Safe Location** or travelling outside one.

---

## 1. Safe Locations

A **Safe Location** is a known and contained area that the DM has explicitly designated as safe for independent activity.

Examples may include:

- A known town or city
    
- A secured settlement
    
- A friendly stronghold
    
- An established party base
    
- Another contained location approved by the DM
    

A Safe Location may still contain dangerous people, criminal activity or isolated encounters. Its designation means that characters can reasonably navigate, operate and regroup within it without triggering the Severing.

The DM determines:

- Whether a location is safe
    
- The recognised boundaries of that location
    
- When a location gains or loses its Safe Location status
    

---

## 2. Splitting Within a Safe Location

While every separated character remains within the boundaries of the **same Safe Location**, the party may split freely.

- There is no penalty for separating.
    
- There is no limit on the number of groups.
    
- A group may consist of a single character.
    
- Characters do not need to remain within sight or hearing of one another.
    
- The Severing does not begin or progress.
    

Before separating, each player or group must clearly state:

1. Where they are going
    
2. What they intend to accomplish
    
3. Their intended end goal
    

Valid objectives may include:

- Resupplying
    
- Shopping or selling items
    
- Visiting a known NPC
    
- Gathering specific information
    
- Visiting a temple, guild or government office
    
- Securing accommodation
    
- Completing personal business
    
- Performing an established downtime activity
    

An open-ended statement such as _“I wander around and see what happens”_ is not a specific objective.

Each group is responsible for tracking its own:

- Location
    
- Objective
    
- Relevant actions
    
- Passage of in-game time
    

---

## 3. Leaving a Safe Location

The Safe Location exemption ends when one or more groups leave its recognised boundaries while the party remains divided.

Outside a Safe Location:

- The party may be divided into a maximum of **three travelling groups**.
    
- A travelling group may consist of only one character.
    
- Each group must have a clearly stated destination and objective.
    
- Each group must state where or how it intends to reunite with the others.
    
- A voluntary action that would create a fourth group is not permitted.
    

The three-group limit applies while travelling through or operating within:

- Roads and wilderness
    
- Dungeons and ruins
    
- Hostile settlements
    
- Unknown territory
    
- Separate encounter areas
    
- Any location not designated as safe by the DM
    

Separation caused directly by the story, an enemy or another unavoidable event may exceed the three-group limit at the DM’s discretion.

---

## 4. The Severing Clock

The **Severing Clock** begins when:

- The party is outside a Safe Location; and
    
- The party is divided into two or more groups.
    

The party has one shared Severing Clock. Separate groups do not receive separate timers.

The clock continues while any travelling group remains separated from the others.

After **one complete in-game day**, the Severing begins imposing supernatural effects. These effects:

- Affect every separated group
    
- Become progressively more severe
    
- Accumulate while separation continues
    
- Do not require saving throws
    
- Cannot be removed while the party remains separated
    

The exact manifestations of the Severing are determined by the DM.

> [!warning] Extended Separation  
> The first 24 hours carry no mechanical penalty. Remaining separated beyond that point becomes increasingly dangerous.

---

## 5. The Thornbound Pull

Once the Severing begins manifesting, every separated character develops a supernatural sense called the **Thornbound Pull**.

The Pull may feel like:

- Pressure behind the heart
    
- Invisible thorns tightening beneath the skin
    
- A tug beneath the ribs
    
- Distant whispers from absent companions
    
- Petals drifting against the wind
    
- Shadows bending in a particular direction
    
- A metallic taste that weakens when moving closer
    

The Thornbound Pull provides every separated group with:

- The general direction of the nearest separated group
    
- Awareness of whether they are moving generally closer or farther away
    
- Updated guidance when the other group changes direction
    

It does **not** provide:

- Exact distance
    
- Exact location
    
- A map or direct route
    
- Knowledge of hazards
    
- The safest available path
    
- Information about the other group’s condition
    

If two groups reunite while a third remains separated, the Thornbound Pull redirects towards the remaining group.

The Severing punishes prolonged separation, but it will not leave the party without a direction towards reunion.

---

## 6. Reuniting the Party

The party is considered fully reunited when either:

- All travelling groups physically reunite outside a Safe Location; or
    
- Every active group enters the same Safe Location.
    

Reuniting only some of the groups does not count as a full reunion.

The following do not count as reunion:

- Magical communication
    
- Sending a familiar or summon between groups
    
- Seeing another group from a distance
    
- One character temporarily moving between groups
    
- Two out of three groups meeting
    
- Different groups entering different Safe Locations
    
- Briefly meeting solely to reset the Severing before separating again
    

When the full party reunites:

- The Severing stops progressing.
    
- Its active supernatural effects subside.
    
- The Severing Clock remains at its current point until properly reset.
    

The clock resets to zero after every active party member completes a long rest while within the **same Safe Location**.

If the party separates again before resetting the clock, the Severing resumes from its previous point.

---

## 7. Final Authority

The DM has final authority over:

- Whether a location is safe
    
- The boundaries of a Safe Location
    
- When travelling groups have formed
    
- Whether an objective is sufficiently clear
    
- Whether the party has properly reunited
    
- How much in-game time has passed
    
- How the Severing manifests
    
- Exceptional or unavoidable separation
    

These rulings follow the established **DM Rulings and Appeals** rules.

---

## Rule Summary

> [!summary]
> 
> - Characters may split freely within the same DM-designated Safe Location.
>     
> - Every group must state its destination, objective and end goal.
>     
> - Outside Safe Locations, the party may form no more than three groups.
>     
> - A group may consist of one character.
>     
> - The Severing Clock begins as soon as the party is divided outside a Safe Location.
>     
> - Supernatural effects begin after 24 hours and worsen over time.
>     
> - Affected groups always retain a general supernatural direction towards reunion.
>     
> - The clock resets only after the entire party reunites and completes a long rest within the same Safe Location.
>
